🎬 How On Location Works
Your guide to exploring filming locations worldwide
🗺️
Explore the Map
Browse 22+ verified IMDb filming locations pinned on an interactive world map. Zoom, pan, and tap any marker to see the story behind it.
🔍
Search
Type any film title, TV show, or place name into the search bar. Results fly the map to the matching location instantly.
🎨
Genre Filters
Use the filter pills below the header to show only Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Action, Drama, or Adventure locations. Color-coded markers match each genre.
📸
Fan Photos
Each location shows a photo grid of shots taken by other visitors. Tap a location marker to open its detail page and see the community gallery.
📍
Check In
Visit a filming location in real life, open it on the map, and tap Check In to earn a ⭐ star. Markers glow gold once you've been there.
🏆
Achievement Badges
Collect stars to unlock 10 film-themed badges — from First Take (1 star) to Hall of Fame (300 stars). A toast notification fires on every unlock.
🌍
Globe Challenge
Check in on all 6 major continents to earn the Around the World in 80 Films badge — the ultimate achievement for the well-traveled cinephile.
🔮
Secret Badge
There's a mysterious 11th badge hidden in your profile's badge grid. Its requirements are unknown. Keep exploring and see what happens…
